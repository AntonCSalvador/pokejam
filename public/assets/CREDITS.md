Each character directory retains upstream `credits.txt` and `AnimData.xml`. `manifest.json` records every image URL, revision, creator, and source terms. The upstream repository's current license is preserved in `SPRITECOLLAB-LICENSE.md`; it is not a claim that the original game assets share that license.

PNG pixels are unchanged. PokéJam adds basketball action sequencing, timing, attachment points, ball animation, court graphics, UI, and effects. Run/Walk poses become dribbling with independent ball bounce; Shoot/Charge poses become passing and shooting; Hop becomes block/dunk, with separate aerial motion. Pokémon belongs to its respective owners. This project is an unofficial fan game.
